AI chat without sending every message to the cloud.

Most AI chat tools depend on remote servers. Pulsagi is built differently: Pulsagi Chat loads open models into your browser, runs inference locally when supported, and sits alongside a growing library of focused browser tools.

  • No signup required to get started.
  • Browser-based chat designed for local-first workflows.
  • Powered by WebGPU and WebLLM where supported.

Why It Feels Different

Built around local-first AI, not cloud-first habits.

Pulsagi Chat is designed to feel premium without hiding the fundamentals. It focuses on privacy, clear behavior, and a setup that respects the browser as the runtime.

Private by Design

Your prompts and chat history are meant to stay inside the browser experience instead of being routed through a hosted chat backend.

Runs in the Browser

No desktop install and no server-side workspace needed. Open the app, load a supported model, and start working.

Smart Model Selection

Pulsagi checks compatibility and surfaces a model list that makes sense for the current browser and device.

Offline-Ready AI

After a model is downloaded into the browser cache, the experience is ready for low-connectivity or offline sessions.

No API Costs

Bring AI chat to the browser without a cloud inference bill attached to every message you send.

Open and Transparent
